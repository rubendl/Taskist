# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** May 2, 2025

This app, "Google Tasks to Todoist Copy Tool", is a personal project used solely by the developer. It accesses your Google Tasks data to copy it into your Todoist account.

## What Data We Access
- Google Tasks (task names, due dates, lists)
- Todoist API (to create tasks)

## How Data Is Used
Data is temporarily used during the copy operation and is not stored or shared in any way.

## Data Retention
No personal data is stored. Everything is processed in-memory only.

## Third Parties
No data is shared with third parties.
